# WorkflowFox NotebookLM Enterprise Showcase Standard

## Purpose

Every WorkflowFox showcase should automatically produce a high-quality enterprise showcase video using NotebookLM.

The objective is not to create marketing videos.

The objective is to create educational engineering showcases that demonstrate Enterprise AI Engineering through production-inspired reference implementations.

NotebookLM is treated as the presentation layer for completed engineering work.

The engineering artifacts remain the primary deliverables.

The video becomes another reusable asset generated from those artifacts.

# Objective

The NotebookLM showcase should explain:

• the business problem

• why the traditional solution evolved

• why scaling becomes difficult

• the architecture

• engineering decisions

• AI-assisted engineering

• validation

• engineering trade-offs

• lessons learned

• future evolution

The audience should understand the engineering thinking behind the implementation rather than simply observing a working application.

# Required NotebookLM Sources

The ideal notebook contains approximately six primary sources.

Required:

✓ README

✓ Case Study

✓ Website Showcase

✓ NotebookLM Showcase Guide

✓ Architecture Pack

✓ Engineering Journal

Optional:

Screenshots

Demo Script

OpenAPI Contract

Do not overload NotebookLM with unnecessary implementation files.

The quality of the showcase is determined by the quality of the engineering documentation rather than the number of uploaded documents.

---

# Custom Topic

Always use a custom topic.

Never rely on NotebookLM's default topic generation.

The custom topic should instruct NotebookLM to create an Enterprise Engineering Showcase rather than a document summary.

---

# Engineering Principles

The showcase should consistently reinforce:

Business before technology.

Architecture before implementation.

OpenAPI before integration.

Validation before completion.

Documentation as a first-class engineering artifact.

AI accelerates engineering.

AI does not replace engineering judgment.

# Success Criteria

A successful WorkflowFox NotebookLM Showcase should leave the audience understanding:

• why the business problem matters

• why the architecture was designed this way

• how AI-assisted engineering was applied responsibly

• how the implementation was validated

• what trade-offs were made

• what lessons enterprise teams can reuse

• how the implementation could evolve into production

The audience should finish with a stronger understanding of Enterprise AI Engineering rather than simply remembering the technologies used.
