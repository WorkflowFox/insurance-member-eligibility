Imagine you're a Provider Relations representative at a healthcare insurance company.

A healthcare provider calls to verify whether a patient is eligible for coverage before scheduling treatment.

At first glance, the request sounds simple.

But answering it often requires searching multiple systems, reviewing coverage information, comparing effective and termination dates, and manually deciding whether the member is currently eligible.

Every individual step is straightforward.

The problem is that the same process is repeated thousands of times every day.

It consumes valuable time, depends heavily on individual experience, and can lead to inconsistent decisions across different representatives.

Our goal wasn't simply to automate a task.

Our goal was to create a reusable eligibility service that provides a consistent, reliable decision while allowing Salesforce to remain the primary user experience for Provider Relations teams.

With that business problem defined, let's look at the solution.
